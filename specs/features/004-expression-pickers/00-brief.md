# Feature Brief: Expression Pickers

> Emoji, symbol, kaomoji, and snippet pickers integrated into the UI.

---

## Problem Statement

Users need quick access to emoji, special symbols, and kaomoji without switching to a browser or external tool.

## Proposed Solution

Tabbed UI with dedicated pickers for emoji, symbols, kaomoji, and user-defined snippets. Recently used items tracked across all pickers.

## Goals

- Emoji picker with Unicode 15.0+ support and category organization
- Symbol picker with math, currency, arrows, etc.
- Kaomoji database with search
- Recently used tracking across all pickers
- Click to copy and optionally auto-paste
